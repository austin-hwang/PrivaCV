# Code of conduct

## Our standard

PrivaCV contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels, give actionable feedback, accept correction gracefully, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/austin-hwang). Do not open a public issue that identifies a target of harassment or includes private information. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing PrivaCV.
